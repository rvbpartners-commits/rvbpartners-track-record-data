# Derived files of the superseded chain

These are the regenerated (non-chained) files that presented the
superseded record: its NAV, its daily profit and loss, its metrics,
its tear sheet, its methodology and its index metadata, exactly as
they stood when the chain was retired.

They are kept for the same reason the snapshots beside them are kept:
the record is not rewritten, it is set aside. The snapshots are the
evidence and they verify from their own genesis; these files are how
that evidence was read.

They are NOT hash-chained -- they never were. Nothing here is
presented: the book's live files are the ones one directory up.
